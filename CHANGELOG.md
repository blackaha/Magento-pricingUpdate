# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-21)

First release, for Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4.4 or later.

- Applies the prices sent by BlackAha, matched to products by SKU, with a result for every update.
- One-click connection to BlackAha, and manual connection with a webhook URL and a signing secret.
- Global and per-website price scope; special prices, tier prices and catalog price rules taken into account.
- Recent price updates listed in the admin, with the reason of every update not applied.
- English.
